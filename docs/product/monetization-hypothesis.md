# Monetization hypothesis

This repository contains a complete free local tool. Monetization is a
hypothesis, not validated demand or revenue proof.

- **Likely buyer:** a small-team HR, safety, training, or credential coordinator
  who maintains a recurring spreadsheet.
- **Free value:** deterministic local CSV-to-calendar generation with no
  account.
- **Possible hosted value:** saved private mappings, scheduled refresh,
  coordinator-only shared calendars, and auditable source-change history.
- **Hypothetical price:** $12–$29 per coordinator per month, subject to buyer
  interviews and willingness-to-pay evidence.
- **Entitlement boundary:** local parsing and calendar export stay free; any
  future recurring synchronization or shared workspace could be paid.
- **Potential usage metric:** successful calendars generated per active
  coordinator, measured only with explicit consent in a future hosted product.

No checkout, payment provider, analytics, hosted service, customer contact, or
pricing test is included.
